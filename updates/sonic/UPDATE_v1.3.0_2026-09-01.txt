UPDATE VERSION: v1.3.0

CHANGES
1. Global Timeline Sync
- Leader broadcasts current round phase and timestamps.
- BETTING uses shared bettingEnds.
- COUNTDOWN uses shared countdownEnds.
- RUNNING uses shared startAt and bust.
- Refresh/rejoin restores the current room position instead of starting a new 10 second local round.

2. Persistent Round History
- Completed rounds are stored in localStorage key igch.sonic.history.v13.
- Up to 250 completed rounds are retained.
- History survives page refresh and browser reopen on the same browser profile.
- Connected peers replicate/merge history through the P2P room.
- Leader sends history snapshot to newly joined peers.

3. History UI
- ROOM badge is clickable.
- Opens ROUND HISTORY modal.
- Shows Round ID, final multiplier and finish time.

4. Room
- SONIC-GLOBAL remains automatic.
- Badge shows REAL count and SOLO / LEADER / SYNC role.

5. Math Safety
- Existing Sonic bust/risk formula remains the source used by the leader.
- RTP, liability, reserve, treasury, payout and cashout formulas are not intentionally changed.

TEST PLAN
A. Open Sonic on two devices and confirm 2 REAL with LEADER/SYNC.
B. During BETTING at approximately 4 seconds, refresh the follower. It must return near the same remaining time, not 10 seconds.
C. During RUNNING, refresh the follower. It must rejoin the same round/multiplier trajectory.
D. Confirm both devices finish at the same multiplier.
E. Open ROOM badge and confirm completed rounds remain after refresh.
F. Open a fresh connected browser and confirm history is replicated from the active room.

LIMITATION
With no permanent backend, a completely new browser cannot recover ancient history if every device that possessed that history is offline. Same-browser persistence and active-peer replication are supported.
