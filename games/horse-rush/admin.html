HORSE RUSH

Cash out before your horse loses the pace.

  1. Join an open race with your stake.
  2. The round starts automatically when the gate closes.
  3. The multiplier rises while the horses accelerate.
  4. Cash out before the hidden stop point.
  5. If the round stops first, your stake is lost.
  6. Auto Cashout and Auto Play can follow predefined rules.

Demo fairness: every round result is locked before the race. House Cash, Reserve and player history do not modify an active outcome.
