Core logic
0) Player wallet starts at 1,000 AZN. Deposits increase the player balance only and remain separate from earned House Cash.
1) House Cash starts from 0 and grows from net player losses.
2) Reserve is emergency capital and is not the same as earned cash.
3) For every accepted round: Liability = Stake × (Multiplier − 1) × SafetyFactor.
4) A bet is accepted only if HouseCash + UsableReserve − LockedLiability can cover that liability.
5) On settlement: HouseCash += Stake − Payout. If result is negative and HouseCash is not enough, only then reserve is used.
6) The engine tries to keep theoretical margin at or above the target by limiting exposure before the bet is accepted — not by changing a result after acceptance.
