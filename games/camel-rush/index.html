How to play

Cash out before the race stops.

  1. Select your stake.
  2. Start the race.
  3. The multiplier rises while your camel runs.
  4. Cash out before the hidden stop point.
  5. If the race stops first, the stake is lost.
  6. Use Auto Cashout and Auto Play for predefined rules.

Demo rule: each round outcome is locked at start. Treasury V2 may adapt the NEXT round probability profile via Effective RTP; it never rewrites an already active round.
